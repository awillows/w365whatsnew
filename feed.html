Subscribe to updates

Get new Windows 365 announcements delivered to Outlook, a Teams channel, or your feed reader.

Feed address — copy this into whichever app you use below.

How to subscribe

Outlook (classic)

  1. In the folder pane, right-click RSS Subscriptions.
  2. Select Add a New RSS Feed.
  3. Paste the feed address and select Add.

The new Outlook doesn't support RSS feeds.

Teams channel

  1. Open the Workflows app in Teams, or Power Automate.
  2. Create a flow using the RSS trigger When a feed item is published and paste the feed address.
  3. Add the action Post message in a chat or channel.

The old RSS connector for Teams was retired with Office 365 connectors.

Feed reader

  1. Open your reader (Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire, Thunderbird, etc.).
  2. Add a new feed and paste the feed address.

Most readers also accept the main site address and find the feed automatically.
